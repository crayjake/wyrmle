# CHAOS counter guide

Checked against the September 26–27 v12 publication. There are 78 counter spellings on the starting board and 299 in the whole finite letter supply. Whole-supply membership does not guarantee that all required letters occur together later.

The hits column is damage before any Revive recovery. A counter can hit each matching enemy letter represented by its selected tiles. Words using two As necessarily use the Revive A on this board. When a word needs only one A, use the Hit A to avoid that recovery. Later in the game, surviving letters and available tiles change the damage.
